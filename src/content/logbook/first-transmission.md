Systems are nominal. The reactor hums, the antenna is warm, and somewhere below,
a small monotreme in a very large helmet is looking out the window at a field of
static that turned out to be stars.

There's nothing to report yet — which is, itself, the report. The point of a
logbook is that it exists *before* you have anything important to say, so that
when you do, there's already a place to say it. Consider this the empty first
page: dated, filed, and waiting.

## What lives here

The Logbook is for **transmissions** — short dispatches from orbit. Dev notes,
half-formed ideas, doodles, the occasional warble into the void. Longer or more
finished things (code, research, the serious cargo) will get their own hold on
the ship later; this is the casual channel.

## How to add one

Drop a Markdown file in `src/content/logbook/`. Give it a `title`, a `stardate`,
and a one-line `summary`, and it shows up here automatically, newest first. Mark
it `draft: true` to keep it grounded until it's ready to fly.
